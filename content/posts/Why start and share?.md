Start with why. I have a genuine aim to help people achieve more through work and life, by making it more visible that everything can be evolved to be better. I spent my career getting to a role where I get to do this daily, and I am looking to find ways to make that bigger nudge to help more people move past perceived constraints. 

So now what? How do I help beyond the scope of my day job, and offer something different?
First off i think its key to recognise that everyone's minds work differently, which is awesome! Its this diversity that makes amazing things happen which keeps life from becoming a grey, mundane, and static. As much as I am currently typing this sentence, I learn far better by doing, experimenting, and adding all the little learning's onto other interconnected learning's floating around in my head. I want to harness and my own experience and nuro-divergence to offer something different. 

So here it is, [[blog/agility/index|Agility]], a literally never completeable resource of inter connecting elements to help me, the teams I work with, and anyone who wants to inspect what is happening in their system and get some inspiration on how to make it better.  This isn't a new approach, "Digital Gardens" / "Second Brains" are a super popular way for individuals to manage their own notes and thoughts. I have build this resource from [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) and I have been loving both the creation and the personal usability of everything been linked together. You may have seen people posting their graph views of how much they have built, and a few even share it completely.

My intent of this site and resource is to strike a balance between being a neutral (ish) reference map to all the incredible things people have done, learned, and shared in the agility space, while also providing some insight from my own experience of what has worked or not worked for me. I think its critical we start talking more about where we as individuals have failed, as this is where we get our greatest learning insights. 

I am purposely pushing myself to share before reaching an ideal MVP or Minimal Viable Release, mostly to let me iterate in an more micro evolutionary way, rather than waiting for it to be "good enough". This likely means anyone who actually reads this when I first post this will find a pile of junk and placeholders. I don't want to waste your time (and sorry if I have) but hopefully it gives you some form of reflection or inspiration either way. 

This post is quite a statement of intent, and while i have all the best intent in the world it will be super interesting to see what the world looks like in a years time and if i have managed to continue to build this towards my aims. 

#### Bonus
It feels important to acknowledge, goal statements like this rarely sound genuine. Hell, I requested and got some critical feedback once and the person questioned me on why I took it so diligently on board, and asked if I was faking it. The majority of the time when we hear people talk about goals they are often preformative sales pitches. I am still new to more public posting and will iterate on it over time, but I do hope in time to show that I do this in my spare time from genuine passion, rather than creating yet more soulless noise.
