"Cool story Will, but why should I care?" 

A brief self-reflection on why I want to post more, and doing so with a purpose. 

I recently attended #AgileOnTheBeach2026 which was epic. Talks on agile practices, how AI is affecting us, and how we can deliver more productivity; it really filled my cup. As someone who wants to help teach others and help us think more deeply about how we do things, I diligently took a thousand notes. After posting two learning logs, I realised I heard but didn't act on the message throughout the talks, which was that action needs to have purpose.

Why am I posting? What am I trying to achieve? And for whom? It's all the same things I challenge others on as a coach, but what about myself?

As  CACI's Agile Practice Lead, I have self-imposed the expectation that I should be doing whatever a "thought leader" does. On the flip side, I genuinely love the subject and spend my own time exploring, learning, and sharing. That's all well and good for me, but is it actually impacting or helping others?

Whenever I unpick it, the reason I do what I do is because I feel most fulfilled by helping others mentally break out of the boxes they've made for themselves. Asking the right question and seeing someone scrunch their face up as some gears start grinding in their head is just amazing. That's a deeper topic (do ask if you want to hear more), but how do I apply this purpose to public posting?

I need to eat my own advice and experiment. I'm going to try and write human stuff that chips away at mental boxes. AI is reality, but I know I resonate the most with human-created content that hasn't been manicured, so let's see how far being a slightly eccentric, rambling (but caring) nutcase gets me.

I guess this post was one of those rambles. Great start 😅
