I did attack what I could with a wire brush to get some of the worst off, but in the end the pain probably offered more protection than me ruffing it all up so left it as is and started creating a new floor!

As I couldn't take the flooring all the way to the end because of the door, I left a a lip. 
Added a metal edge to attempt to protect everything as it goes in and out. I left the flap to help water run off too.

Later I added a solid beam to the remaining space and attached bike mounts. I used bolts so if I ever need to reset the mounts or remove it then its only a allen key away.
