Wanted some pzazz, and since i have no intention of trying to sell the van at any point there is no downside to having a go, beyond it looking terrible. 

All done with a sharpie. Took inspiration from my tattoo and canvas my sister made me, and pretty much just had at it. going so large means that unless you are up close you don't notice that the lines are not neat at all. 

The pen has done a great job of staying on, though its not UV resistant so elements on the front of the van parked in the sun degrade and need re-doing about twice a year, and the back more sheltered once.
