## Overview
>[!quote] Scrum is a lightweight framework that helps people, teams and organizations generate value through adaptive solutions for complex problems.

#### Source 
**Source**: [The Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html#scrum-definition) 
**Authors**: Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland

#### Ownership
The core definition of Scrum ( [The Scrum Guide](https://scrumguides.org/scrum-guide.html#scrum-definition)) is licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode) 
Being an open framework there are multiple groups, organisations and bodies associated with the framework that offer a range of further offerings such as materials, events, certifications, training and more. 
The core and root organisations in this space are [Scrum Alliance](https://www.scrumalliance.org/) and [Scrum.org](Scrum.org), associated with Ken and Jeff respectively. 

Being "the most popular framework for implement agile", nearly every organisation in the delivery space provides some form of associated offering or adaptation of scrum.  Its fair to say that not all these organisations are created equal and many apply their own philosophies,  values and practices that diverge from the ones defined in the scrum guide to varying degrees.  Aside from specific trademarked titles and products there is nothing stopping anyone from selling and self certifying a "scrum" course or certificate.
