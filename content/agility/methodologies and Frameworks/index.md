Methodologies and Frameworks

## Methodologies and Frameworks #todo 
In short;
**Methodology** - A systematic approach on how to do something. Usually covers how to do everything to achieve the aim.
**Framework** - A structure to support in how to doing something. Provides guidance often as a mix of values, principles and practices

Its more nuanced, but you could view methodologies as the way something must be done, and frameworks as a tool to help

The difference between these terms is often missed because the majority of peoples day jobs don't focus on understanding systems, so are used interchangeably. True business agility lurks in these differences and the amount of space either provides to adapt.
