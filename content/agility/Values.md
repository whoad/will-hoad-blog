## Definition
Values are core things we care about, that  guide our decisions and actions. 

When people come together and share their values with each other, the alignment often becomes the basis of a friendship group, community, all the way up to Political Parties, Religions and cultures.  

All the things we create inherently embody the values they were instilled with.  

## Why values?
Values are used as a high level guide to achieve an outcome.  They are often the most impactful part of a system as they act as a foundation that all other elements work towards. The idea being that all the tangible and practical parts  within a methodology or framework work together to achieve the values.  E.g. If you value being fit, you may take dietary and exercise actions to achieve it.  

Values are great for covering the fuzzy space around more tightly defined practices.

## Conceptual levels
* **Mindset** - Deep Unconscious behaviour 
* **Values** - Subjective beliefs & opinions, what we want, 
* **Principles** - Objective reality, what we should do, Universal / self-evident
* **Practices** - Practical
* **Processes & Tools** - Component parts
